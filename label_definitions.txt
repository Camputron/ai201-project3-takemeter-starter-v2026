evidence: backs its point with something a reader can check without trusting the poster — a link, a specific number or date, a named source or quote, or a concrete technical mechanism or instruction
anecdote: backs its point with the poster's own first-hand experience — what they did, used, saw, or counted themselves — and offers nothing checkable beyond that
unsupported: gives an opinion, judgment, joke, question, or reaction with no support offered
